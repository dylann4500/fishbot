Every measurement in this section comes from the artifacts listed in Appendix~\ref{app:repro} and is

Two of the three supra-floor rungs are recovered with a \emph{lower bound} above the
\vsevenPrimaryFloor{} floor. The third, the \texttt{0.08} rung, recovers \vsevenBnineHeightExcess{}
$[\vsevenBnineHeightExcessLo, \vsevenBnineHeightExcessHi]$ --- above the floor on the point estimate
but with an interval that straddles it, so on the lower-bound reading this paper applies to its own
primary claim it is \emph{not} recovered (\vsevenBnineHeightRecovered{}). Its planted cost,
\vsevenBnineHeightCost{} $[\vsevenBnineHeightCostLo, \vsevenBnineHeightCostHi]$, straddles the floor
too, so the rung is only supra-floor on a point estimate in the first place. The recovered size does
track the planted size monotonically across all four rungs, and the ladder is sharply non-linear,
with most of the damage arriving between the second and third. The sub-floor rung is \textbf{not} recovered ---
\vsevenBnineHfiveExcess{} $[\vsevenBnineHfiveExcessLo, \vsevenBnineHfiveExcessHi]$, below the floor with an
interval containing zero --- which is the control that has to fail and does.

Two qualifications belong beside it. An earlier phase measured this same excess at \vsevenSubFloorExcess{}
$[\vsevenSubFloorExcessLo, \vsevenSubFloorExcessHi]$ and this evaluation gets
\vsevenBnineHfiveExcess{}: the same conclusion from a point
estimate seven tenths of a point higher. And its upper bound \emph{grazes} the floor. The verdict is
unchanged --- the test is whether the edge is recovered, and it is not --- but the margin is thinner
than the earlier phase's, and a reader entitled to the caveat is entitled to it here rather than in a
footnote.

The identity control is exact: \frozen{} against itself is \vsevenIdentityRate{} with a zero-width
interval on both banks over \vsevenIdentityN{} games each (\vsevenIdentityExact{}). The fitted responders lose to \emph{unhandicapped} \frozen{} in all four rows, reproducing
\S\ref{sec:exploit}'s finding from a different fitting bank and against a handicapped target. The
objective is \emph{not} independent --- these responders and four of the eight adversary searches
both maximise win rate --- so this is a second sample, not a second method. And the three planted side channels each fail exactly the
tests the protocol names for them and no others (Table~\ref{tab:sidechannel}).
