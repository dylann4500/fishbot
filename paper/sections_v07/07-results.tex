\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

Every number in this section comes from the artifacts listed in
Appendix~\ref{app:repro} and is emitted into this document by
\filepath{engine/build\_tables\_v07.py --paper}; none is typed by hand. The battery is
\vsevenScoredCells{} scored cells over \vsevenScoredGames{} games with
\vsevenAuditViolations{} audit violations and \vsevenLimitCells{} cell carrying any action-limit hit.

The order of this section is fixed by the project's reporting rule and not by what flatters the
release: \textbf{the worst case and the minimax regret come first, the aggregate comes last and is
labelled a diagnostic, and the partner-regime table appears whether or not it helps.}

\subsection{The negative controls}
\label{sec:results-controls}

If these misbehave, nothing else in this report is a measurement.

\begin{table}[t]\centering

\label{tab:controls}
\input{tables_v07/controls}
\end{table}

All three supra-floor rungs are recovered above the \vsevenPrimaryFloor{} floor and the recovered size
tracks the planted size monotonically; the ladder is sharply non-linear, with most of the damage
arriving between the second and third rungs. The sub-floor rung is \textbf{not} recovered ---
\vsevenBnineHfiveExcess{} $[\vsevenBnineHfiveExcessLo, \vsevenBnineHfiveExcessHi]$, below the floor with an
interval containing zero --- which is the control that has to fail and does.

Two qualifications belong beside it. An earlier phase measured this same excess at $-0.01$
$[-0.45, +0.42]$ and this evaluation gets \vsevenBnineHfiveExcess{}: the same conclusion from a point
estimate seven tenths of a point higher. And its upper bound \emph{grazes} the floor. The verdict is
unchanged --- the test is whether the edge is recovered, and it is not --- but the margin is thinner
than the earlier phase's, and a reader entitled to the caveat is entitled to it here rather than in a
footnote.

The identity control is exact: \frozen{} against itself is \vsevenIdentityRate{} with a zero-width
interval on both banks over \vsevenIdentityN{} games each (\vsevenIdentityExact{}). The fitted
responders lose to \emph{unhandicapped} \frozen{} in all four rows, independently reproducing
\S\ref{sec:exploit}'s finding from a different fitting bank and a different objective. And the three
planted side channels each fail exactly the test built to catch them
(Table~\ref{tab:sidechannel}).
