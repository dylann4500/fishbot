\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The v0.7 programme set out to find whether the \vsix{} policy class is flat or merely badly measured.
It built an instrument, characterised what that instrument can see, generated adversaries in the open,
built five candidate architectures, froze one configuration, and then measured it against material
sealed before the configuration existed under a protocol that said in advance what would count as
failure.

\textbf{The class is flat, and the evidence for that is the shape of what survived.}

One configuration is a certified advancement over the \vsix{} frontier:
\vsevenBtwoFcheap{}~points $[\vsevenBtwoFcheapLo, \vsevenBtwoFcheapHi]$ against \fcheap{} over
\vsevenBtwoFcheapN{} games of sealed material, replicating on both banks, gate-clean, and with a
worst cell on the shared \vsevenPanelMembers{}-member panel (\vsevenWorstFrozen{}) far better than
the incumbent's \vsevenWorstIncumbent{}. Nothing built to exploit it
succeeds --- eight independent searches, the white-box inverter included, every one losing, the largest
upper bound \vsevenBfourWorstUpper{}. It is not a private convention: a single seat's upgrade is worth
\vsevenOneSeatFive{}~points of the \vsevenSoneSelf{}-point three-seat gain, partner substitution
leaves a minimum of \vsevenSoneMin{}, and three independently-trained runs cross-play within
\vsevenXpGapAbs{}~points of their own diagonal.

\textbf{And it adds nothing measurable to the configuration phase~2 already had}
(\vsevenBtwoComposite{} $[\vsevenBtwoCompositeLo, \vsevenBtwoCompositeHi]$). The gain that exists was
found by an unfitted coordinate sweep, not by architecture work. The three phases of architecture work
that followed produced four kills and one configuration change. The one quantity that falls short of
its training value is the group of keys the freeze was selected over, by
\vsevenSelWorstShortfall{}~points against an expected maximum under the null of
\vsevenSelExpectedMax{}. And the protocol's own test for whether the gain is located does not replicate
across banks.

So the contribution of this cycle is the closed directions and the named barrier, and the protocol
said in advance that this would be a legitimate deliverable rather than a failure to apologise for.

\paragraph{The closed directions.} The declaration-allocation channel --- the register's second-ranked
entry --- is worth exactly zero, its named mechanism shown not to exist over
$\sim$\vsevenLoneDecls{} recorded declarations, its proposed fix shown to be worse than the object it
would replace, and most of its residual error class shown to be an information limit. A leaf evaluator
fitted \vsevenKoneFold$\times$ better in the only statistic its consumer can use buys nothing, because
that consumer is nearly leaf-invariant. Fitting against any self-oriented per-decision objective
produces a worse policy while moving its own proxy the right way, because the two per-decision channels
trade against each other. Nothing learned survives a free random tie-break inside the exchangeable tie
group, whose selection signal is $\sim$\vsevenCurseShare\% winner's curse. Two further directions
closed on arithmetic before they were built. None of these is a null anyone need re-run.

\paragraph{The barrier.} It is not that the mechanisms were badly built --- three of the four moved
their own proxies in the intended direction and one improved its fit statistic by
\vsevenKoneFold$\times$. It is that \emph{this policy class converts mechanism into win rate at a rate below the
instrument's floor}, and the floor does not buy down with evaluation games. The one thing that did
convert was a single coordinate found by sweeping, not by designing. A programme that wants to move
this policy further has to change one of the two terms: build a search that can find effects below
\vsevenPrimaryFloor{} points, or find out whether a dozen quarter-point mechanisms compound. This cycle
did neither, and the last one did not either, and both said so.

\paragraph{What should not be claimed.} No policy in this corpus is near-optimal, and none is known to
be the strongest Fish-playing team, because the comparison class has one member and every
exploitability number in the corpus is a lower bound from a search rather than a bound on what a search
could find. \S\ref{sec:towardone} lists what would have to be measured before either sentence could be
written. It is a list of experiments, not of opinions, and none of them has been run.
