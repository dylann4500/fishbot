WHY IT MOVES ANYTHING AT ALL.  feasibleAllocation runs on every candidate half-suit,
not only the one declared.  The joint argmax is provably identical on the sets that
are actually declared; the disagreements live on low-confidence sets that are NOT
declared, where the rescored `prob` perturbs the declare/don't-declare comparison.
So jalloc is inert on the decision L1 names and active only on a different decision,
where it is measurably (if barely) harmful.

6. WHAT PHASE 6 SHOULD TAKE FROM THIS
================================================================================
* L1's named mechanism (marginal product vs joint posterior) is closed at exactly
  zero, on ~210,000 declarations, on two banks, in both urgency arms.
* L1's channel is real but its addressable part is an INFORMATION LIMIT under the
  uniform-deal prior, and after urgency deletion its whole oracle ceiling (0.84 pts)
  sits below the 1.53-point detection floor.
* The only remaining route into the flat states is the POLICY PRIOR -- who asked for
  what, which the uniform-deal posterior discards by construction.  Shipped accuracy
  in flat states is 0.573 (urgency on) / 0.686 (urgency off) against a provable 0.500
  coin flip, so the deployed marginals already extract some of it.  That is L11's
  territory, not L1's, and it is worth at most 0.46 points post-urgency-off.
* Exact Bayesian inference is not a ceiling in this game.  Measured here for the
  third time in the corpus and the first time on the declaration decision itself.
